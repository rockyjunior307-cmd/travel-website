Our Story

A Local Company You Can Trust

NUZA TOUR was founded by passionate Lembongan locals who wanted visitors to experience the islands exactly as they do — authentically, safely, and unforgettably. Since 2018, we've guided thousands of travelers through the crystal waters and lush landscapes of Nusa Lembongan and Nusa Penida.

  • Locally owned and operated by Nusa Lembongan islanders
  • Certified guides with deep knowledge of local culture, marine life, and terrain
  • Commitment to sustainable, eco-friendly tourism
  • Personalized service — no overcrowded group tours
  • All-inclusive packages with no surprise charges
